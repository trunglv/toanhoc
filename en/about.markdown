These are notes on the parts of mathematics I find most interesting, written the way I wish they had been explained to me in school: start from a question, draw the picture first, then write the formula.

Every post tries to answer three things: **what it is**, **why it's true**, and **what it's good for**. If a step made you pause, that's usually the part worth expanding.
